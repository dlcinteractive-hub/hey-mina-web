Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open MinaSpark and enter your email address. We'll send you a 6-digit code. Enter the code to sign in — no password needed. Then create your child's profile by entering their first name.

How do I add spelling lists?

Go to the Word Lists tab and tap the + button. Give your list a name (like "Week 5" or "Sight Words"), then add words by typing them one at a time. You can also scan a printed word list using the camera button.

How does the camera scan work?

Tap the camera icon on any word list. Take a photo of a printed spelling list or worksheet. MinaSpark uses image recognition to read the words and lets you review them before adding to the list.

How do I switch between children?

Go to the Profile tab. You'll see all children under your account. Tap a child's name to switch to their profile. Each child has their own word lists, math progress, and stars.

How does math facts practice work?

The Math tab shows a 12x12 multiplication grid colored by mastery level. Tap "Start Practice" and answer timed multiplication questions. The app tracks which facts your child has mastered, is learning, or is struggling with, and focuses practice on the ones that need work.

What are stars and daily challenges?

Stars are earned during practice sessions for correct answers and streaks. Daily challenges are special goals that refresh each day — like "Complete a spelling session" or "Get a perfect score." Completing challenges earns bonus stars.

How do I reset progress?

To reset a spelling list's mastery data, open the list and look for the reset option. To start completely fresh, you can delete and recreate a child profile from the Profile tab. Note: deleting a profile removes all their data permanently.

Is MinaSpark free?

MinaSpark is free to download and use. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no premium tiers.

What ages is MinaSpark for?

MinaSpark is designed for children ages 5-12, with parents managing the account. The spelling lists are fully customizable so you can match your child's school curriculum at any level.
